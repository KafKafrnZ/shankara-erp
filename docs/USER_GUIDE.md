# Finding and managing the item catalog

Plain-language guide for anyone who will use Shankara ERP. No technical
background needed. IT staff should read [`DEPLOYMENT.md`](DEPLOYMENT.md)
instead.

---

## What this is

The item catalog for Shankara Buildpro — SKU list (codes, brands, HSN,
groups) that Tally exports produce, searchable in a browser instead of
flipping through Excel.

Anyone with a login can search it. Only a **steward** can add, edit, or
upload items.

Tally is still where accounting lives. This system does not post anything
back to Tally.

---

## How to open it

Ask IT for the exact address once it is live. It will look like:

```
https://erp.shankara.local
```

Bookmark it. You come back to the same link every time.

**This only works on the office network** (office Wi‑Fi, or a cable at a
desk). It is an internal system, like an intranet page — not a public
website. From home or another city it will not load unless IT has given
you remote access into the office network. That is expected.

The first time, your browser may warn that the certificate is not from a
public company. IT installs a one-time trust on your PC so the padlock
looks normal after that. Do not click through warnings on a machine IT
has not set up.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Steward** | Search, upload new item files, add/edit/remove items, manage other people's logins |
| **Finance** | Search and view only |
| **Branch** | Search and view only |

If you need steward access, ask someone who already has it. On their
side it is a short form under **People**.

---

## Logging in

Your username is your work email. Your password is given to you by a
steward when the account is created. It is never the same as anyone
else's, and it is never written in a shared document.

**Change your password** while signed in: click **Password** in the top
bar. You will need the current one.

**Forgot your password?** There is no email reset. Ask an office admin
to set a new one from the People screen.

After about eight hours you will be asked to sign in again. That is
normal.

---

## A few rules

- **Never share your login.** Every action is tied to whoever is signed
  in. Someone else's mistake lands under your name.
- **Log out on shared computers.** Closing the tab is not the same as
  logging out.
- **Stewards: only upload a file you have checked.** “Merge into catalog”
  makes it visible to everyone immediately.
- **Something look wrong?** A search returning nothing you expect, an
  error, a page that will not load — screenshot it and send it to IT.
  Do not try to fix the server yourself.
